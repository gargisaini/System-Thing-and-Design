### Evidence markers used in P34
`[E]` = behaviour our research documented · `[H]` = hypothesis/proposal · `[P]` = prototype · `[I]` = inference. LIKELY = "the adaptation already happens under today's arrangement [E]"; POSSIBLE = "plausible from the mechanism, no direct evidence".

### Key terminology (exact)
Mechanisms M1 "Claims persist", M2 "Status is unreadable", M3 "Access runs on asking", M4 "Problems surface late; correction is blunt" (p3; worded "Misuse surfaces as a breakdown" on p6), M5 "Allocation follows history and funding".
Leverage points: LP1 Correction Targeting · LP2 Loop Gain on Hoarding · LP3 Information Flow · LP4 Rules of Engagement · LP5 Self-Organization Capacity · LP6 System Goal · LP7 Mental Model.
Principles: P1 Visibility first · P2 Target the actor · P3 Claims decay · P4 Route the need · P5 Actual usage · P6 Attribution + sharing · P7 Self-serve.
Interventions: 1 Claim Signals · 2 Default-Decay Claims · 3 Proportionate Care Protocol · 4 Resource Stewards + Escalation Ladder · 5 Periodic Allocation & Issue Review.
Prototypes: P1 desk claim card · P2 written rule · P3 proportionate care · P4 decision-rights matrix · P5 escalation ladder. (Note: "P1–P5" here are *prototypes*, not phases or principles. The slides say "Prototype P1" etc. to avoid the clash.)
Card states: IN USE · AWAY UNTIL · FREE TO BORROW TODAY · PROTECTED SETUP · ASK FIRST · LAST HERE.
Numbers: 7 / 21 / 5-day desk rule · 14-day AWAY max · continuity exception ≤4 weeks steward / >4 weeks faculty · 2-h GPU lapse · deadline ≤72 h · steward cap 1 h/week · 45-min termly review · issue logged 3+ times goes on the review agenda · review date within 4 weeks for a blanket restriction.
